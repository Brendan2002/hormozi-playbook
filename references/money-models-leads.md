# Money Models and Leads reference

Paraphrased menus from Hormozi's Money Models and Leads. Use them to make Build, Money model and Leads outputs concrete. Pick the few that fit; don't list them all back.

## Money model: pick one from each stage
**Attraction offer** (gets a stranger in, ideally at or near break-even).
- Win-your-money-back: buyer earns the refund by doing the actions that produce the result.
- Giveaway: free prize or sample with a qualifying step that filters buyers from freebie hunters.
- Decoy: a deliberately worse option that makes the target option look obvious.
- Buy X, get Y: bundle a low-cost add-on to lift value without cutting price.
- Pay less now or pay more later: discount for committing up front, or a premium for deferring.

**Upsell** (right after the first yes).
- Classic: a bigger or better version offered at the moment of purchase.
- Menu: show a short list of add-ons.
- Anchor: show a high-priced option first so the target looks cheap.
- Rollover: credit what they've already paid toward a larger package.

**Downsell** (for those who say no).
- Payment plan: same offer, spread out.
- Trial with penalty: try it, with a small cost if they walk away.
- Feature downsell: strip features to hit a lower price.

**Continuity** (recurring revenue).
- Continuity bonus: a perk that only arrives while they stay.
- Continuity discount: lower price for staying.
- Waived fee: drop a fee for staying a set time.

**Math check.** Gross profit from a new customer's first 30 days vs. cost to acquire them. Show: price, cost to deliver, gross profit, acquisition cost, payback in days. Label every assumption. Run the arithmetic.

## Leads
**Rule of 100.** Commit to 100 outreach actions a day (or a set daily number you can sustain) for a long enough stretch to learn what works, then improve in order: more volume, then better, then new channels.

**Lead magnet types** (one narrow problem, solved for free, pointing to the paid offer).
- Reveal a problem they didn't know they had.
- Give one step of a multi-step process.
- A sample (fits Moozy literally).
- A done-for-you slice of the work.
- A diagnostic or scorecard.
- A short trial.

**Content: hook, retain, reward.** Hook in the first line, hold attention with a reason to keep reading, pay off with a useful takeaway.

**Lead getters.** Referrals, employees, agencies, affiliates/partners. For Moozy, store managers and event organizers who refer others count as partners.

**30-day plan skeleton.** Week 1 set the daily number and tracking sheet; week 2 run the best-fit Core Four channel; week 3 review results and cut the weakest; week 4 double the strongest or fix its message. See templates.md for the table.
